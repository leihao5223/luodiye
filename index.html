Android / iPhone 处理步骤 Android / iPhone Resolution Steps

下面的内容是安全自查思路，不要求在这个页面输入任何验证码或提交任何账号信息。 These are self-check steps only. This page does not ask for any verification code or account submission.

Android

应用内检查 In-app review
01

打开设置并查看已关联设备 Open settings and review linked devices

优先检查当前账号是否存在你不认识的设备、浏览器或桌面会话。 Start by checking whether your account has any device, browser, or desktop session you do not recognize.

02

退出陌生会话并保留自己的设备 Sign out unfamiliar sessions

如果发现异常活动，只保留你自己正在使用的设备，其余全部退出。 If you detect suspicious activity, keep only your current trusted device and sign out the rest.

03

检查两步验证与恢复邮箱 Review two-step verification and recovery email

确认安全设置是你自己开启的，并检查恢复邮箱是否正确、可用。 Make sure the security settings were enabled by you and that your recovery email is valid.

04

仅根据应用内或官方页面继续操作 Continue only from the app or official pages

如果系统要求进一步确认，请只参考应用内提示或官方帮助中心，不要通过陌生页面输入验证码。 If further confirmation is required, rely on the app or official help articles and do not enter codes on unknown pages.

iPhone

安全自查 Safety check
01

在设置中检查账号状态 Check account status in settings

先在应用内查看账号信息、已关联设备和近期是否有异常提醒。 Inspect account details, linked devices, and recent alerts directly in the app.

02

移除不熟悉的设备或网页登录 Remove unknown devices or web sessions

如果设备名称、系统版本或最近活动与你本人不符，请及时退出。 If a device name, system version, or recent activity does not match your own use, sign it out immediately.

03

检查通知与应用版本 Check notifications and app version

确认你没有错过任何安全通知，并将应用更新到最新版本。 Ensure you have not missed important security notices and update the app to the latest version.

04

遇到问题时使用官方帮助中心 Use the official Help Center if needed

如果你无法确认账号状态或恢复访问，请通过官方帮助文章继续处理。 If you still cannot confirm account status or regain access, continue with official help articles.

有人向你索要验证码 Someone asks for your code

无论对方如何解释，验证码都只应由你本人在官方应用内使用。 No matter how it is explained, verification codes should only be used by you inside the official app.

🛑
陌生链接要求你立即验证 An unknown link demands urgent verification

请先关闭链接，回到应用内或官方帮助中心核对是否真的需要处理。 Close the link first and verify from the app or official Help Center before taking any action.

🔒
突然出现陌生设备 A new device appears unexpectedly

及时退出异常设备，并检查你自己的安全设置是否被更改。 Sign out the device immediately and confirm that your own security settings were not changed.

常见问题 Frequently asked questions

这里回答一些与安全验证、自查和官方帮助路径有关的基础问题。 These answers cover basic questions about safety checks, verification prompts, and official support paths.

为什么页面不提供验证码输入框？ Why is there no code entry form here?

因为验证码只能在官方应用内或官方授权流程里由你本人使用，这个页面只提供安全指引与官方资源导航。 Because verification codes should only be used by you inside official flows. This page is guidance-only.

如果收到陌生人的“协助验证”消息怎么办？ What if someone offers to “help verify” my account?

停止交流，不要发送验证码、截图或设备信息，直接回到应用内或官方帮助中心核对。 Stop the conversation, do not send codes, screenshots, or device details, and verify from official channels instead.

我已经退出陌生设备，还需要做什么？ I signed out an unknown device. What next?

继续检查两步验证、恢复邮箱、应用更新状态，并留意后续是否还有异常提醒。 Review two-step verification, recovery email, app version, and watch for any further unusual activity.

需要继续处理账号安全问题？ Need to continue with account safety checks?

请优先返回应用内设置或打开官方帮助中心，不要在陌生页面提交验证码、账号或设备信息。 Return to in-app settings or open the official Help Center. Do not submit verification codes, account details, or device info on unknown pages.
